## What the Closing Door Prank trend is

In the real phone videos, a fixed camera looks through a doorway into a bright hallway. One person stands by a half-open door with a hand on the handle. The other waits at the far end, then runs. Just before they reach the frame, the door swings shut. A beat later the runner is on the camera side laughing — or the door opens and both of them react. Some posts swap roles and do a second run in the same clip.

By the end of October 2026, family hallway remakes were stacking millions of views. There is no single “official” song; people reuse whatever sound was under the post they copied. The AI wave that followed is simple: keep that door timing from a template, put your faces on the two roles, skip the physical stunt.
